Maintenance

Planned servicing keeps systems running and avoids a failure on the hottest day. We strip units down, clean out the dust lodged deep inside, fix or replace what’s worn, then test before we leave. Call us when you notice:

  • No cool air, or slow cooling
  • A smell when the system runs
  • A noisy unit, or one that won’t turn on
